# Contribution and impact evidence

Use alongside manuscript-shape.md before method selection, before drafting, and in independent evaluation. A readable, correct paper is not automatically an important contribution. Explicit user objectives and scientific integrity take precedence; do not inflate claims to reach a target score.

## Build a project contribution dossier

The methodology architect owns `contribution_dossier.md` in the project root. In an existing-paper revision, first establish it from the current manuscript and primary sources. Literature-lead updates source findings before drafting; the modeler/validator supplies evidence; the independent grader challenges the dossier. All writing and review dispatches receive its path. Keep it compact and outside the scientific article.

Record:
- Intended article type, specific reader/decision problem, strongest proposed claim, and the intellectual or practical result the reader would lose if the paper did not exist.
- A balanced benchmark set: relevant author papers from the vault/source pack AND published work by other groups. Include the closest methodological precedent, an exemplar of consequential practical work, and a useful explanatory exemplar; one paper can serve several roles. Choose by relevance and demonstrable contribution, not venue prestige or citation count. When external originals are unavailable, record the access limit instead of claiming a completed benchmark.
- For each benchmark, exact version and section/page/equation, what it already establishes, what makes its contribution effective, and what should or should not transfer. The vault is an index; read original sources. A positive exemplar does not replace a search for closer or contradictory work.
- A claim-to-precedent table: proposed claim; closest verified result; precise difference; status (established / new derivation or analysis verified here / candidate unverified); supporting proof, code or empirical result; remaining uncertainty. Label a new calculation separately from a novel method. No source search establishes priority merely because nothing was found.
- An impact chain: intended user -> concrete decision/inference -> existing alternative -> change enabled -> meaningful consequence -> supporting evidence -> limits and adoption burdens. Identify the simplest evidence that would disprove the claimed benefit.

## Create for contribution, not for score

Before drafting, compare plausible contributions, including a focused synthesis if that is the honest genre. Prefer a specific consequential question to an inventory of topics. Specify the claim and decisive evaluation before selecting favorable examples. Add the smallest substantiated analysis that addresses the gap; replace weak or repetitive exposition rather than append every idea.

A method winning the objective it was optimized for verifies implementation, not external usefulness. Compare credible alternatives under common information/constraints, show tradeoffs and failure settings, and explain the meaning of the effect size. For a perspective/synthesis, assess whether it resolves a real ambiguity and yields a defensible usable recommendation; do not demand gratuitous new theorems or simulated superiority. For a new-method claim, require a defensible difference from the nearest method and evidence of what that difference enables.

Clinical or policy labels require grounded inputs. Distinguish observed data, assumed prior distributions, elicited preferences, and illustrative costs. If inputs are hypothetical, test transparent ranges and retain that qualification. Do not present expected counts in a hypothetical trial population as predicted patient benefit. Real data alone, extra pages, or complex algebra do not establish impact.

## Evaluate independently and route gaps

The grader reads primary benchmark passages and the finished argument, not just the writer's positioning. Report three separate conclusions: (1) technical/editorial readiness; (2) contribution relative to the nearest work; (3) potential impact and strength of supporting evidence. Give locators and the strongest counterargument. Publication success is not a guarantee that an exemplar deserves a numerical 4–5 on every dimension. No automatic novelty floor for importing tools across disciplines.

Use a contribution status: SUPPORTED (for the declared genre and bounded claim), PROVISIONAL (specific unresolved source or evaluation gap), or NOT_ESTABLISHED. State whether the impact is illustrated, empirically supported, or observed in use; adoption is not required for a strong potential-impact case, but a plausible story alone is not evidence.

Execution passes and aggregate scores cannot override an unresolved central contribution gap. Before drafting, make the decisive research plan explicit; validate new analysis before incorporating it. After drafting, route proof/data/method gaps back to the architect and validator within the existing authorized scope and remaining rethink budget. The fixer may improve exposition and attribution but cannot manufacture novelty. If new work is not authorized, infeasible, or the budget is exhausted, preserve the best verified version and report the gap/next experiment. Do not silently relabel a requested new-method paper as a tutorial or run no-op polishing rounds. A source-supported change of genre is acceptable when consistent with the user’s aim and explicitly recorded.

The final report must say what became stronger and what remains unproved. Success means the declared contribution is supported in its scope, not journal acceptance or human scientific sign-off.
